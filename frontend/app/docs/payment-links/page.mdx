
# Payment Links

Payment Links are the fastest way to accept payments with Coal. They allow you to sell products or accept donations without writing a single line of code.

> [!NOTE]
> Payment Links are perfect for no-code use cases, newsletters, social media, and direct messages.

## How it works

1.  **Create a Link**: Go to the **Console**, navigate to **Payment Links**, and click "Create New".
2.  **Configure**: Choose a specific product to sell, or leave it open for donations (flexible amount).
3.  **Share**: Copy the generated URL (e.g. `usecoal.xyz/pay/my-product`) and send it to your customers.

## Use Cases

### Selling Products
If you have a fixed price item (like the "Super Coffee" in our demo), you can link a Payment Link directly to a **Product**. When the user visits the link, they will see the product details and price automatically.

### Accepting Donations
If you select "Donation" or leave the product field empty, the user can enter any amount they wish to pay. This is ideal for:
*   Content creators accepting tips.
*   Open source projects.
*   Charity causes.

## Customization

Payment Links automatically use your **Merchant Profile** settings (Name, Logo) to brand the checkout page, ensuring a trusted experience for your users.
